Research dimensions

The same five categories, every time, so a company's story is comparable across tickers and across the weeks you keep coming back to it. Each score is deterministic, against fixed and documented thresholds; the optional AI layer only narrates what the data shows, on both sides, and never invents a number or tells you what to do.

Growth

Revenue and earnings growth, scored against fixed thresholds.

Profitability

Margins and return on equity that show how efficiently the company operates.

Valuation

Trailing P/E, price-to-book, and other multiples relative to the business.

Financial Health

Debt levels and liquidity, so leverage risk isn't hiding off to the side.

Momentum

Price trend and position within its 52-week range.

See the full research brief

Every category score is traceable to a specific metric and threshold. With AI narration on, you also get a written thesis, argued on both sides, and a confidence level.
