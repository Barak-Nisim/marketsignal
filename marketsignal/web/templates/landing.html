$ marketsignal --features

  1. Real financial data. Pulls live fundamentals and price history from Yahoo Finance. No sample data, no fabricated numbers.
  2. Five deterministic signals. Valuation, Growth, Profitability, Financial Health, and Momentum, each scored against fixed, documented thresholds. Explainable, not a black box.
  3. AI-reasoned thesis. Claude synthesizes the signals into a written thesis with a confidence level. It narrates the signals; it never invents or overrides them.
  4. What changed since last time. Every run is saved locally. The next time you check a ticker, the report shows exactly what moved since your last look.

$ marketsignal --why

Real research means pulling up ten different numbers across three tabs and trying to hold the whole picture in your head, every single time you check a name. MarketSignal turns that into one pass: the same five categories, the same thresholds, every time, so a stock's story is comparable across tickers and across the weeks you keep coming back to it. The AI layer adds reasoning on top of the numbers; it never replaces them.
