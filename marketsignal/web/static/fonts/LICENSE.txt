Fonts in this directory are self-hosted (no CDN) to keep the app fully
self-contained, and are used under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1:
https://openfontlicense.org

IBM Plex Mono and IBM Plex Sans by IBM, sourced from Google Fonts
(https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Mono,
https://fonts.google.com/specimen/IBM+Plex+Sans).

IBM Plex Sans is distributed here as a single variable-weight file
(plexsans-variable.woff2) covering weights 400-700; IBM Plex Mono ships
as four separate static-weight files, matching how each family is
actually served upstream.

Both are redistributable and embeddable under the OFL. No attribution
is required within the rendered app; this notice is kept here for
license traceability.
